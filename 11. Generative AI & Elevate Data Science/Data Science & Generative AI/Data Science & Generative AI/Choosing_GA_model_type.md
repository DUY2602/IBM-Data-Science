# Guide to Choosing a Generative AI Model Type

### Types of generative AI models

| Model                                  | Key features                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Applications                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Generative adversarial networks (GANs) | 1. Two competing neural networks: generator and discriminator.1. The generator learns to create realistic data, while the discriminator learns to distinguish real from fake.1. The adversarial training process continuously improves both networks.1. Can be challenging to train and achieve stable results. | 1. Image generation: faces, landscapes, objects1. Text generation: poems, code, scripts1. Video generation: realistic videos, animation1. Drug discovery: generate molecules with intended properties1. Music generation: composing new songs             |
| Variational autoencoders (VAEs)        | 1. Encode input data into a lower-dimensional latent space1. Learn a probability distribution over the latent space1. Decode samples from the latent space to generate new data points1. Focuses on learning a meaningful representation of the data                                                            | 1. Image compression: efficiently stores and transmits images1. Anomaly detection: identify unusual data points1. Dimensionality reduction: compress high-dimensional data1. Text summarization: generate concise summaries of text documents             |
| Autoregressive models                  | 1. Generate data point by point, conditioned on previously generated points1. Use recurrent neural networks (RNNs) or transformers to capture long-term dependencies1. Can be computationally expensive for long sequences                                                                                      | 1. Text generation: realistic and coherent text sequences1. Music generation: generating music that follows genre and style1. Time series forecasting: predicting future values of a time series1. Image inpainting: filling in missing parts of an image |
| Diffusion models                       | 1. Start with a simple noise and gradually "de-noise" it into realistic data1. Use a U-Net architecture with skip connections to preserve information1. Can be more stable and easier to train than GANs, but often slower                                                                                      | 1. Image generation: high-quality and diverse images1. Text generation: coherent and grammatically correct text1. Audio generation: realistic and musical audio1. Inpainting and denoising: improving the quality of images or audio                      |
| Flow-based models                      | 1. Transform a simple distribution (Gaussian) into a complex one using invertible transformations1. Learn the parameters of these transformations from the data1. Can be efficient and accurate for high-dimensional data, but training can be challenging                                                      | 1. Image generation: realistic and diverse images1. Density estimation: modeling the probability distribution of data1. Dimensionality reduction: compress high-dimensional data1. Anomaly detection: identify unusual data points                        |

### Comparison of models on different considerations

| Feature                 | GANs                                        | VAEs                                       | Autoregressive models                          | Diffusion models                      | Flow-based models                        |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Data type               | Images, text, audio                         | Images, text, continuous data              | Images, text, sequences                        | Images, text                          | Images, continuous data                  |
| Task objective          | High-fidelity generation, data augmentation | Encoding/decoding, representation learning | Sequence generation, text-to-image translation | Image generation, editing, inpainting | Image generation, conditional generation |
| Quality of samples      | High-fidelity, diverse                      | Often blurry, less realistic               | Sharp, high-resolution                         | High-fidelity, diverse                | High-fidelity, controllable              |
| Control over generation | Limited                                     | Moderate                                   | High                                           | Moderate                              | High                                     |
| Training complexity     | High                                        | Moderate                                   | High                                           | Moderate                              | High                                     |
| Interpretability        | Low                                         | Moderate                                   | High                                           | Moderate                              | Low                                      |
